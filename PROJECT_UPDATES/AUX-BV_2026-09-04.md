## Quartic factor conventions

With corrected `U=(Re p)^2`, define

`F1 = -t^2 U - (1+t)^2 epsilon`,

`F2 = (1+q)^2(1+t)^2 - 4tU`,

`F3 = q(1+t)^2(q-t^2-2t)^2 - t^2(q-3t)^2 U`.

The physical-domain Bessel inequality gives `epsilon >= 0`. The exact identities

`(1+t^2)^2 L_- = t(1-t)^4 + epsilon(4t^4+2t^3+4t^2+2t+4) + t epsilon^2`,

`(1+t^2)^2 L_+ = t(1+t)^4 + epsilon(4t^4-2t^3+4t^2-2t+4) + t epsilon^2`

show `L_->0` and `L_+>0` for `0<t<1`.

The Schur ellipse gives `U < (1+q)^2`, hence `F2>0`. The conic gives the denominator-free identity

`(1-t)^2 L_- F3`

`= (1+t)^2 L_+ [ q(1-t)^2(q+t^2-2t)^2 + t^2(q-3t)^2 V ]`,

so `F3>=0`. Since `F1<=0`, the factor product `(-F1) F2 F3` is nonnegative.

## Raw squared-defect reconstruction

For positive charge singular values `mu1>=mu2>=0`, the reconstructed raw formula is

`Dgap := (mu1^2+mu2^2-1/4)^2 - 4 mu1^2 mu2^2`

`= - 4 t^3 (3-qt)^2 G^2 / [q^6 (1+t)^6 L_+^3 Delta^2] * F1 F2 F3`,

where

`G = q^2 t - 2q t^2 - 2q + 3t`,

`Delta = (1-t^2)^2 - (1-t)^2 (Re s)^2 - (1+t)^2 (Im s)^2`.

An independent reconstruction from the raw numerator metric/residue formulas agrees with the direct charge calculation on generic normalized quartics to floating-point error (~`1e-15`). ## Unsquaring / Gate-A link

The exact algebraic identity

`Dgap = ((mu1-mu2)^2-1/4) ((mu1+mu2)^2-1/4)`

is elementary. The already-proved degree-four Gate-A energy bound gives

`5/4 <= 2(mu1^2+mu2^2)`.

Therefore the second factor is strictly positive. Once `Dgap>=0` is established, the first factor is nonnegative and the ordering `mu1>=mu2` yields

`mu1-mu2 >= 1/2`.

This makes the **quartic charge-gap theorem the correct next promotion**, provided the raw squared-defect bridge is included in a restartable exact audit and the paper uses the corrected `p`-coordinate convention.
